Safety rules built into this prototype
• Never pay an upfront fee to get a job.
• Never accept gift cards, cheque overpayments or requests to forward money.
• No automatic application or messaging; the person reviews everything first.
• Regulated/high-risk work is flagged instead of recommended blindly.
• Exact home address should not be exposed before both sides agree to the job.
• Source link stays visible so the worker can verify the original posting.
Prototype for validation only. It does not guarantee work, pay, availability, licensing, safety, or that a posting remains active. Users must verify the original posting and comply with local employment, tax, licensing and safety rules.
